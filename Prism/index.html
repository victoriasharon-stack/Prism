Field notes on the light

Mirrors

A ray leaves at the angle it arrived, mirrored across the surface normal.

0 out = 0 in

Prisms

Each face bends light by Snell's law. A ray inside that meets a face too steeply reflects instead.

n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂

Colour

This glass has index 1.42 for red up to 1.68 for blue, so white light fans into five bands. Real flint glass spreads far less.

n 1.42 right arrow 1.68

Lenses

A thin lens tips a ray toward its axis by its height over the focal length, so a fan can meet again as white.

triangle's slope = -h / f
